Explore Our Treatments

Personalized solutions for preventive, restorative, cosmetic and emergency dental needs.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Advanced non-surgical gum care using modern LASER technology. Scaling removes plaque and tartar for moderate cases, while deeper periodontal concerns may receive root planing and laser-supported treatment.

Tooth Filling

We restore cavities and damaged teeth using high-quality materials and precise techniques, preserving natural tooth structure and protecting against future decay.

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth cannot be saved, extractions are performed gently and with careful explanation, comfort-first techniques and appropriate recovery support.

Artificial Complete Denture

Precision-crafted complete dentures restore function and aesthetics with durable materials, natural appearance and a comfortable, customized fit.

Dental Implants

Biocompatible titanium implants provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth, restoring natural appearance, chewing strength and long-term confidence.

Laser Dentistry

Advanced laser procedures enable precise, minimally invasive soft-tissue and gum treatments with faster healing and reduced discomfort.

Root Canal Therapy

Infected pulp is carefully removed, the canal is cleaned and sealed, relieving pain, eliminating infection and preserving the natural tooth.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Impacted or problematic wisdom teeth are removed using modern techniques and anesthesia options, with complete post-operative care.

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

Custom bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring restorations to adjacent teeth or implants, improving appearance, chewing and bite stability.

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening safely lightens external and internal stains caused by food, beverages, age and other factors for a brighter smile.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells cover chips, gaps and discoloration to create a natural-looking, balanced and attractive smile.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, comprehensive dental care helps children feel safe during check-ups and preventive treatments, establishing healthy habits for life.

Flap Surgery

For advanced gum disease, gum tissue is carefully lifted to remove deep plaque and bacteria, then repositioned to support optimal healing.

Orthodontic Braces

Metal, ceramic and lingual braces are customized to correct misalignment, improve bite function and create a confident smile.

Clear Aligners

Transparent, removable aligner trays gradually straighten teeth with comfort and flexibility for a discreet orthodontic option.
