About our clinic

Get to Know Dr. Anupriya

Your trusted partner in modern, compassionate dental care at Hosur.

Our founder

Two decades of trusted expertise

Bringing over 20 years of expertise, Dr. Anupriya stands as a leading figure in modern dentistry at Hosur. She began her professional journey after graduating in 2000 and refined her clinical skills during six impactful years at Mathura Clinic.

In 2004, she established Sakthi Dental Clinic with a clear vision: to make high-quality dental care accessible to all. She has also served for over a decade as a trusted dental consultant at the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital.

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, advanced dental technology meets a warm, patient-friendly environment. Every visit is personalized, comfortable and stress-free.

Our mission

Personal care for every smile

We are committed to delivering personalized, compassionate and advanced dental services in a welcoming environment. Continuous learning and innovation guide every preventive, restorative and specialized treatment.

Our vision

A healthier, confident community

We aspire to set new standards in patient care, innovation and community engagement by encouraging preventive practices, building trust and making dental outcomes that support lifelong health and confidence.
