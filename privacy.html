Privacy Policy

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store and disclose information from visitors and users of our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information when users visit our website, fill out forms, or engage with features and services. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Users may visit anonymously. We collect personal data only when it is voluntarily submitted. Refusing to provide certain information may limit access to some features.

We may also collect non-personal information such as browser type, device details, operating system, internet service provider and technical data about site interaction.

2. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance the user experience. Cookies are small files stored on a user’s device for record-keeping and tracking preferences or activity. Users can configure their browser to refuse cookies or alert them when cookies are being used. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and manage the website and ensure content and services run smoothly
  • To improve customer service and respond to inquiries efficiently
  • To enhance the website experience based on feedback
  • To communicate through email regarding inquiries, appointments or service updates

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures for data collection, storage and processing to safeguard personal information from unauthorized access, alteration or destruction.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent personal identification information to third parties. We may share general demographic data that is not linked to personal information with trusted partners or affiliates to improve service delivery and site performance.

6. Children’s Privacy

In compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our site is not intended to attract users below this age.

7. Changes to This Policy

Sakthi Dental Clinic may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

By using this website, you agree to this policy. If you do not accept our Privacy Policy, please do not use our website. Continued use following updates will be deemed acceptance of the revised policy.
